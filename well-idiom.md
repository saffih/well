# WELL Idiom

Express significant instructions, definitions, decisions, and constraints together with their reasons, because future changes must preserve the intent rather than merely reproduce the wording.

Keep relevant conditions, exceptions, assumptions, and trade-offs explicit and close to what they qualify.

When revising or reorganizing text, preserve the underlying intent, protections, and meaningful distinctions. Reconsider choices when their reasons or circumstances change, rather than treating existing wording as permanently binding.

Prefer concise, self-contained statements. Avoid unnecessary explanation, artificial justification, and rigid formatting.

A stated reason explains intent; it does not establish that the reasoning is correct.

This is a writing convention, not a formal document-conformance standard. It requires no special structure, references, mandatory keywords, or checker.
